Notion AI vs a Plain ChatGPT Sidecar: Which Actually Saves Time?

2026-09-12 · ToolTested

Notion AI is convenient, but convenience is not the same as speed. I ran my actual work notes — meeting notes, project pages, a personal wiki of about 300 pages — through both setups for two weeks and timed the annoying tasks.

Setup A: Notion AI (the built-in)

The good parts are real:

  • Summarize a meeting note inside the page: 2 clicks, no context switching. Winner.
  • Q&A across your workspace: ask "what did we decide about pricing?" and it searches your pages. When your notes live in Notion, nothing else does this.
  • Autofill properties: it tags and titles database entries as they're created.

The bad part is the price for anything else. The AI writing features — draft an email, rewrite this paragraph — are the same model you can use in a free chatbot, just billed per member per month.

Setup B: the plain chatbot sidecar

Everything else happened in a chat window: drafting, rewriting, brainstorming, research. The friction is copy-paste. You lose 20-40 seconds per task to moving text around, and you lose findability — the output doesn't live next to the source note.

The hybrid that beat both

After two weeks the winning setup was:

1. Keep notes and databases in Notion, AI writing off unless your team shares the workspace

2. Use Notion AI only for in-place actions: summarize this page, Q&A my wiki, autofill

3. Use a separate free chatbot for anything generative, and paste results into Notion as a "cannon" you actually file

For a solo user with one workspace, the math is brutal: the subscription costs more per month than the seconds it saves. For teams whose knowledge lives in Notion, workspace Q&A alone pays for it.

Verdict

Notion AI is a retrieval product pretending to also be a writing product. Buy it for retrieval, skip it for writing.
